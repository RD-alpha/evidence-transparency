# Tax Avoidance Promoters Policy Paper
S0001 Who is likely to be affected Promoters of avoidance schemes who seek to circumvent the Promoters of Tax Avoidance Schemes ( POTAS ) legislation through the use of associated and successor entities.
S0002 General description of the measure This measure will ensure that promoters of tax avoidance schemes cannot circumvent the POTAS regime by re-organising their business so that they either share control of a promoting business or put a person or persons between themselves and the promoting business.
S0003 This will ensure HM Revenue and Customs ( HMRC ) can apply the POTAS regime as intended when introduced.
S0004 Policy objective The changes to this legislation deter the use of avoidance schemes through influencing the behaviour of promoters, their intermediaries and clients.
S0005 Background to the measure Changes to the POTAS legislation were introduced in Finance Act ( FA )
S0006 2015 to ensure promoters cannot use associated and successor entities to circumvent the legislation.
S0007 This measure will ensure these associated and successor entities rules function as intended.
S0008 Detailed proposal Operative date This measure will have effect on and after 8 March 2017.
S0009 Current law Current legislation for POTAS can be found in Part 5 and Schedules 34 to 36 to Finance Act ( FA )
S0010 2014 (as amended).
S0011 Proposed revisions Legislation will be introduced in Finance Bill 2017 to amend the control definitions in paragraph 13A of Schedule 34 to FA 2014.
S0012 These amendments introduce the term ‘significant influence’ to ensure promoters cannot reorganise their business so that they put a person or persons between themselves and the promoting business.
S0013 The amendment also ensures that the control definitions apply where 2 or more persons together have control or significant influence over a business.
S0014 Consequential amendments will be made in paragraphs 13B to 13D of Schedule 34.
S0015 These are only consequential amendments and will not change how these paragraphs operate.
S0016 Equivalent amendments will be made to the corresponding paragraphs in Schedule 34A.
S0017 Summary of impacts Exchequer impact (£m)
S0018 2017 to 2018 2018 to 2019 2019 to 2020 2020 to 2021 2021 to 2022 This measure is not expected to have additional Exchequer impact.
S0019 This measure supports the Exchequer in its commitment to protect revenue that was set out in the original Budget 2013 costing for POTAS .
S0020 More details can be found in the policy document published alongside Budget 2013.
S0021 Economic impact This measure is not expected to have any significant economic impacts.
S0022 Impact on individuals, households and families The measure will impact on individuals who promote tax avoidance.
S0023 This measure is not expected to impact on family formation, stability or breakdown.
S0024 Equalities impacts This measure will affect individuals who are likely to share protected characteristics with others of above average means.
S0025 It is anticipated that equality groups represented in lower income groups are less likely to be affected.
S0026 Impact on business including civil society organisations This measure will have no impact on businesses and civil society organisations who are undertaking normal commercial transactions; it will only impact on the businesses that are engaging in or promoting tax avoidance.
S0027 Operational impact (£m)
S0028 ( HMRC or other)
S0029 There will be no significant operational impact arising from this measure.
S0030 Other impacts Other impacts have been considered and none have been identified.
S0031 Monitoring and evaluation The measure will be monitored using information collected from the limited population of designated promoters and their users.

Summary
S0097 1.
S0098 This clause amends the Promoters of Tax Avoidance Schemes (POTAS) legislation in Schedule 34 and Schedule 34A of the Finance Act (FA) 2014.
S0099 It ensures HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) can issue conduct notices as intended by the POTAS regime in specified situations where a person has, or persons together have, control or significant influence over a relevant body.

Background note
S0145 18.
S0146 The POTAS legislation was introduced in FA 2014 and aims to change the behaviour of a small and persistent minority of promoters of tax avoidance schemes who display certain behaviours.
S0147 19.
S0148 Where any one of a number of conditions are met, HMRC must consider whether to give the promoter a conduct notice lasting for up to two years.
S0149 A conduct notice imposes conditions on how that promoter must behave.
S0150 If the promoter breaches the conditions of the conduct notice, HMRC may apply to the Tribunal for approval to give the promoter a monitoring notice.
S0151 Monitored promoters are subject to additional information requirements and penalties for non-compliance with those requirements.
S0152 20.
S0153 These changes ensure that the POTAS legislation functions as intended and are part of the government’s strategic response to tax avoidance to deter the development and use of avoidance schemes through influencing the behaviour of promoters, their intermediaries and clients.
