S0132 This information is very important but although the shareholders are the legal owners of
S0133 the company, they may not be the ultimate owners or controllers of the company.
S0134 Companies may be owned through a chain of companies with an ultimate controller at the top, or individuals may appoint a nominee to hold their shares for them so that they don’t appear on a shareholder register.
S0135 These people are the beneficial owners of the company.
S0136 If they have a high level of interest or control, they can materially influence the way the company is run.
