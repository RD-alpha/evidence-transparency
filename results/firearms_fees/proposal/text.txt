S0559 Option 1: Do nothing There has been media and Parliamentary criticism of the public subsidising firearms licences administered by the police, at a time when public spending needs to be reduced.
S0560 These fees were increased to full cost recovery in April 2015.
S0561 Without a power to charge for prohibited firearms licences, the taxpayer and existing fee payers will subsidise this aspect of licensing to an increasing degree as costs increase over time.
S0562 Option 2: Introduce a new set of enabling powers to allow fees to be charged There is a risk that small companies whose trade involving prohibited weapons is low value could stop this aspect of business activity if profit is not high enough to justify the cost of the licence.
S0563 Equally, museums and shooting clubs whose activities requiring a firearms licence are of low value to the public or club members may stop those activities because the cost of the licence is not justified.
