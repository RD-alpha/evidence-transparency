S0047 Local Infrastructure Rate 2.1 Public Works Loan Board The Local Infrastructure Rate would be accessed through the Public Works Loan Board ( PWLB ) or its successor body.
S0048 The PWLB is an independent statutory body that issues loans to local authorities, and other specified bodies, from the National Loans Fund.
S0049 Nearly all borrowers from the PWLB are local authorities requiring loans for capital purposes.
S0052 The policy on rates and repayment terms for PWLB loans is the responsibility of the Treasury.
S0053 Monies for PWLB loans are drawn from the National Loans Fund, which is the government’s main borrowing account and is administered by the Treasury.
S0054 The interest rates on loans provided by PWLB are directly linked to gilt yields.
S0061 2.2 Details on Local Infrastructure Rate More details on the precise requirements for the Local Infrastructure Rate would be set out in due course.
S0063 Bids would be considered from principal authorities, the Greater London Authority and its functional bodies, Integrated Transport Authorities and combined authorities for projects in the transport, energy, flood defences, water, waste, and digital communications sectors.
S0064 As is currently the case, ultimate responsibility for projects, including financing, assessment and delivery, remains with council members, who are democratically accountable to their electorate.
S0065 In order to allocate amounts of discounted lending for projects, the government will need a clear and transparent process to assess bids.
S0066 The government’s current proposal is that councils would be required to produce analysis and an estimate of Net Present Value for the project, signed off by the Section 151 Officer, in line with Green Book appraisal guidance.
S0067 Full details would be set out in due course, but this could include an estimate of net present value, a high level business case, a borrowing profile and a risk assessment.
S0068 Councils would be required to submit their bids to the government, and the government would approve bids that provide evidence of high value for money.
S0069 Successful bidding authorities would be given an allocation of borrowing at the Local Infrastructure Rate.
S0070 This allocation would then be accessible via the PWLB until the body is abolished, after which loans would be made by its successor.
S0071 The Local Infrastructure Rate would only be available to finance the projects for which allocations were given.
S0072 It would not be available for other non-approved projects, or to refinance existing debts.
