S0001 Who is likely to be affected Promoters of avoidance schemes who seek to circumvent the Promoters of Tax Avoidance Schemes ( POTAS ) legislation through the use of associated and successor entities.
S0005 Background to the measure Changes to the POTAS legislation were introduced in Finance Act ( FA )
S0006 2015 to ensure promoters cannot use associated and successor entities to circumvent the legislation.
S0146 The POTAS legislation was introduced in FA 2014 and aims to change the behaviour of a small and persistent minority of promoters of tax avoidance schemes who display certain behaviours.
S0148 Where any one of a number of conditions are met, HMRC must consider whether to give the promoter a conduct notice lasting for up to two years.
S0149 A conduct notice imposes conditions on how that promoter must behave.
S0150 If the promoter breaches the conditions of the conduct notice, HMRC may apply to the Tribunal for approval to give the promoter a monitoring notice.
S0151 Monitored promoters are subject to additional information requirements and penalties for non-compliance with those requirements.
