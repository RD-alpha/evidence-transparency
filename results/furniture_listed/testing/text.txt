S0530 In line with government policy (and the approach taken in relation to technical standards), and to reflect good practice and the need for legislation to keep pace with technological change, the draft Regulations include a five year review clause.
S0531 This will ensure that the Regulations are explicitly reviewed within five years to ensure they remain fit for purpose, and are re-confirmed or updated as required.
S0532 We would not envisage the first review to be a substantial exercise as we are unlikely to have a significant body of evidence on the operation of the new Regulations, but this will provide an opportunity to reflect on the practical application of the legislation.
