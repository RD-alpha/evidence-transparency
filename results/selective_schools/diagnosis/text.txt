S0419 Faith designated schools and academies are allowed to prioritise children of their faith when they are over-subscribed.
S0420 Voluntary-aided faith schools (including those which have converted to become academies) are able to select up to 100% of pupils on the basis of faith.
S0421 Many faith schools choose to open up a proportion of places to children of other faiths or none, and some do not apply any faith criteria at all.
S0423 For new academies and faith schools that have been opened under the free schools programme, a ‘cap’ was introduced on faith-based admissions in order to support inclusivity and tolerance, meaning that when the free school is over-subscribed it can only apply faith-based oversubscription criteria to the first 50% of places.
S0425 The effect that this has had in promoting inclusion and community cohesion is, however, questionable.
